Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Overview

72 Names ("the App") is a Kabbalistic meditation app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

The App collects and stores the following data locally on your device only:

• Birth date — used to calculate your personal Sacred Name based on the Kabbalistic calendar. Stored in Android DataStore on your device.

• Language preference — detected from your device settings to display content in your language.

Data We Do NOT Collect

• We do not collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, or location.

• We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

• We do not transmit any data to external servers.

• We do not use cookies or similar technologies.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore. No data is sent to or stored on remote servers. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all stored data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages as it contains only spiritual and educational content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
